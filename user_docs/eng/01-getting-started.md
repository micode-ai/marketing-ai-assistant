# Getting Started

## What is Marketing AI Assistant?

Marketing AI Assistant is an AI-powered marketing automation platform that helps you create content, manage campaigns, send emails, and analyze results — all in one place. The platform uses artificial intelligence (GPT-4o) to generate marketing materials tailored to your brand and audience.

## Creating Your Account

### Register with Email

1. Open the application at **http://localhost:5173**
2. Click **Sign Up** on the login page
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** — your full name
   - **Email** — your email address
   - **Password** — at least 8 characters
   - **Organization Name** — your company or team name
4. Click **Register**

After registration, you'll be automatically logged in and redirected to the Dashboard. Your account comes with a **14-day free trial** of the FREE plan.

### Sign in with Google

1. Click **Sign in with Google** on the login page
2. Select your Google account
3. Grant the required permissions
4. You'll be redirected back to the application

If this is your first time, an organization will be created automatically using your Google account name.

### Logging In

1. Open the application at **http://localhost:5173**
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Click **Sign In**

## Dashboard Overview

After logging in, you'll see the **Dashboard** — your central hub for managing marketing projects.

## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Create a Project

1. Click **New Project** on the Dashboard
2. Fill in project details:
   - **Name** — e.g., "Spring Product Launch"
   - **Description** — brief project overview
   - **Website URL** — your website (optional)
   - **Target Audience** — who you're targeting
   - **Industry** — your business sector
3. Click **Create**

### Step 2: Generate Content with AI

1. Open your project
2. Go to the **Content** section
3. Click **Generate with AI**
4. Select:
   - **Content type** — Social Post, Blog Article, Email, etc.
   - **Platform** — Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, etc.
   - **Topic** — what you want to write about
   - **Tone** — professional, casual, humorous
5. Click **Generate**
6. Review and edit the generated content
7. **Publish** when ready

### Step 3: Create a Campaign

1. Go to the **Campaigns** section
2. Click **New Campaign**
3. Set:
   - **Name** — campaign name
   - **Type** — Email, Social, Blog, or Multi-channel
   - **Start/End dates** — campaign timeline
   - **Budget** — optional budget amount
4. Add content to your campaign

### Step 4: Set Up Email Marketing

1. Go to **Settings > Email Accounts**
2. Add your email account (SMTP or Resend)
3. Go to your project's **Email** section
4. Create an **Email List**
5. Add **Subscribers**
6. Compose and **Send** your campaign

## Language Settings

The application supports three languages:
- **English** (default)
- **Polish**
- **Russian**

To change the language:
1. Look for the language switcher in the navigation
2. Select your preferred language
3. The interface will update immediately

Your language preference is saved automatically.
