# AI Features

## Overview

Marketing AI Assistant uses OpenAI's GPT-4o model to help you create marketing content, generate documents, build checklists, and provide strategic advice. All AI features are designed to work with your project context — your brand voice, target audience, and industry — to produce relevant, on-brand outputs.

## AI Content Generation

### How It Works

1. Go to your project's **Content** section
2. Click **Generate with AI**
3. Configure the generation parameters:
   - **Content Type** — what kind of content to create
   - **Platform** — which social network or channel
   - **Topic** — the subject matter
   - **Keywords** — relevant terms to include
   - **Tone** — writing style (professional, casual, humorous, formal)
   - **Length** — short, medium, or long

4. Click **Generate**
5. The AI creates the content using your project's brand guidelines
6. Review, edit, and publish

### Content Types

| Type | Description | Best For |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Social Post | Short-form social media content | Twitter, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram |
| Blog Article | Long-form articles with sections | Company blog, Medium, content marketing |
| Email | Email marketing copy | Newsletters, promotional emails |
| Newsletter | Periodic email content | Weekly/monthly updates |
| Ad Copy | Advertising text | Google Ads, Facebook Ads, LinkedIn Ads |
| Landing Page | Web page content | Campaign landing pages |

### Tips for Better Results

- **Fill in project details** — The more context you provide (target audience, brand voice, industry), the better the results
- **Be specific with topics** — "Q1 2026 SaaS product launch for enterprise CTOs" produces better results than "product launch"
- **Use keywords** — Include important terms that should appear in the content
- **Iterate** — Generate multiple versions and combine the best parts
- **Edit the output** — AI provides a strong starting point; always review and personalize

## AI Checklist Generation

### How It Works

1. Go to your project's **Checklists** section
2. Click **Generate with AI**
3. Select the checklist type:
   - **Launch** — product/feature launch preparation
   - **Weekly** — recurring marketing tasks
   - **Campaign Prep** — pre-campaign setup
   - **SEO** — search engine optimization
   - **Social Media** — social management
   - **Email Campaign** — email preparation

4. Add optional context describing your specific needs
5. Click **Generate**

The AI creates a prioritized checklist with tasks relevant to your project and chosen type.

### What You Get

- Task items with clear, actionable descriptions
- Priority assignments (Low, Medium, High, Critical)
- Logical ordering of tasks

## AI Document Generation

### How It Works

1. Go to your project's **Documents** section
2. Click **Generate with AI**
3. Select the document type:
   - **Marketing Plan** — comprehensive strategy document
   - **Report** — performance or analysis report
   - **Competitive Analysis** — competitor comparison
   - **Brand Guidelines** — brand identity documentation
   - **Content Calendar** — content scheduling plan

4. Specify the topic and any additional context
5. Click **Generate**

### What You Get

- Structured Markdown document with headers and sections
- Actionable recommendations tailored to your project
- Data-driven insights based on your project context

## AI Chat Assistant

### How It Works

1. Click **AI Chat** in the main navigation
2. Type your question or request
3. The AI responds with marketing advice and guidance

### What You Can Ask

- **Strategy questions**: "What marketing channels should I focus on for a B2B SaaS launch?"
- **Content ideas**: "Give me 10 blog post ideas for a fintech startup"
- **Email help**: "Write a subject line for our Black Friday campaign"
- **SEO advice**: "What keywords should I target for organic traffic?"
- **Campaign planning**: "How should I structure a 3-month content marketing plan?"
- **Analytics insights**: "What metrics should I track for email marketing?"

### Context-Aware Chat

If you select a project when starting a chat, the AI loads your project's context:
- Project description and goals
- Target audience information
- Brand voice preferences
- Industry specifics

This means the AI can give you advice specifically tailored to your project.

### Multi-Language Support

The AI Chat supports conversations in:
- **English**
- **Polish**
- **Russian**

Write in your preferred language, and the AI will respond in the same language.

## AI Usage Tracking

Every AI generation is tracked:
- **Agent Runs** — view all AI generations for your project
- **Status** — see if a generation is pending, running, completed, or failed
- **Token Usage** — track how many AI tokens were used
- **Cost** — estimated cost of each generation

View your AI usage in the project's Analytics or Agent Runs section.
