# Team Management & Billing

## Team Management

### Organization Roles

Every user belongs to an organization with a specific role:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| **Owner** | Full access. Can manage billing, delete organization, remove any member |
| **Admin** | Manage team members, projects, and settings. Cannot delete organization |
| **Member** | Access projects, create content, view analytics. Cannot manage team |

### Inviting Team Members

1. Go to **Settings > Team**
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter:
   - **Email** — the person's email address
   - **Role** — Member or Admin
4. Click **Send Invite**

The invited person will receive an email with instructions to join your organization.

### Removing Team Members

1. Go to **Settings > Team**
2. Find the member you want to remove
3. Click the **Remove** button
4. Confirm the action

Only Owners and Admins can remove members.

### Team Limits by Plan

| Plan | Team Members |
|------|-------------|
| FREE | 1 (only the owner) |
| PRO | Up to 5 |
| ENTERPRISE | Unlimited |

## Billing & Subscriptions

### Available Plans

#### FREE Plan
- 1 project
- 50 AI generations per month
- 100 emails per month
- 1 team member
- 3 documents per month
- Basic checklist templates
- No brand voice or A/B testing

#### PRO Plan
- 5 projects
- 500 AI generations per month
- 5,000 emails per month
- 5 team members
- 30 documents per month
- 3 integrations
- All checklist templates
- Brand voice customization
- A/B testing

#### ENTERPRISE Plan
- Unlimited projects
- Unlimited AI generations
- 50,000 emails per month
- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited documents
- Unlimited integrations
- All + custom checklist templates
- Brand voice customization
- A/B testing
- API access
- Priority support

### Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**
2. View your current plan and usage
3. Click **Upgrade** next to the desired plan
4. You'll be redirected to Stripe's secure checkout
5. Enter your payment information
6. After payment, your plan is upgraded immediately

### Managing Your Subscription

1. Go to **Settings > Billing**
2. Click **Manage Subscription**
3. You'll be redirected to Stripe's billing portal where you can:
   - View invoices
   - Update payment method
   - Change plan
   - Cancel subscription

### Free Trial

New accounts receive a **14-day free trial**:
- Full access to FREE plan features
- No credit card required
- After 14 days, you continue on the FREE plan limits

### Cancellation

To cancel your subscription:
1. Go to **Settings > Billing**
2. Click **Manage Subscription**
3. In the Stripe portal, click **Cancel Plan**
4. Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period

## Organization Settings

### Updating Organization Details

1. Go to **Settings > Organization**
2. You can update:
   - **Organization name**
   - **Logo** (URL)
   - **Slug** (URL-friendly identifier)
3. Click **Save**

### Email Account Settings

1. Go to **Settings > Email Accounts**
2. View and manage connected email accounts
3. Add new accounts for sending campaigns
4. Remove accounts you no longer use

### Integration Settings

1. Go to **Settings > Integrations**
2. Connect third-party services
3. Manage API keys and credentials

## Account Settings

### Updating Your Profile

1. Click on your avatar/name in the navigation
2. Go to **Profile Settings**
3. Update your:
   - **Name**
   - **Avatar**
4. Click **Save**

### Changing Password

Contact your administrator or use the password reset flow on the login page.

### Google Account

If you signed up with Google, your account is linked to your Google profile. You can always sign in using the **Sign in with Google** button.
